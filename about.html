Welcome to OM Restaurant

"One cannot think well, love well, sleep well, if one has not dined well."

Rooted in culinary devotion, OM Restaurant has been Khurja’s trusted destination for authentic 100% pure vegetarian dining. In our tradition, food is sacred — prepared with royal heritage recipes, freshly ground tandoor spices, and pure desi ghee to bring families and friends together.
