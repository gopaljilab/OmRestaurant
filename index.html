About Us

Welcome to OM Restaurant

For over 15 years, OM Restaurant has been the cornerstone of pure vegetarian dining in Khurja, blending royal Mughlai curries, fragrant basmati rice dishes, and traditional tandoori delights prepared with farm-sourced produce and pure desi ghee.

Food in our tradition is holy. We honor this sacred bond with uncompromising kitchen hygiene, artisanal ground masalas, and warm family service that turns every meal into a celebration.
